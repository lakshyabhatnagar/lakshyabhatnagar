## About Me

I am an undergraduate engineering student at **Netaji Subhas University of Technology, Delhi**, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning and backend engineering.

- AI/ML Intern at **Pumpumpum**
- Pursuing B.Tech in **Instrumentation and Control Engineering** at NSUT
- Chairperson of the **IEEE Power & Energy Society Chapter, NSUT**
- Former ML-IoT Intern at the **India Meteorological Department**
- Building production-oriented systems around **LLMs, RAG, NLP and machine learning deployment**

My work focuses on taking AI systems beyond notebooks by building retrieval pipelines, APIs, backend services, evaluation workflows and deployable full-stack applications.

## Current Focus

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation and hybrid search
- Agentic and LLM-powered applications
- FastAPI-based AI backend systems
- NLP and semantic retrieval
- Production machine learning pipelines
- AI system evaluation and observability
